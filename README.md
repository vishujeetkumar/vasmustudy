# vasmustudy
VasmuStudy is a free, open-access educational platform built for students of the Bihar Board and beyond. Founded in Nalanda, Bihar by Vishujeet Kumar, the platform was created to eliminate the resource gap that forces talented students in small towns and villages to struggle due to expensive coaching or locked study materials.

Key Features & Offerings

• 100% Free & No Paywalls: Completely free access with no subscriptions, hidden charges, forced logins, or data collection.
• Distraction-Free Reading: Designed specifically for mobile and desktop reading without intrusive popups, banners, or corporate clutter.
• Comprehensive Subject Library: Features chapter-wise notes, line-by-line explanations, glossaries, practice tests with model answers, and visual learning aids across subjects:
	• Class 12 English (Prose, Poetry, and Read, Think & Enjoy)
	• Hindi Literature
	• Mathematics (13-chapter library)
	• Biology, Physics, and Chemistry
• Exam-Focused Content: Carefully written, human-curated resources tailored directly to Bihar Board syllabus patterns rather than auto-generated or copy-pasted filler.

Mission & Philosophy

Grounded in the belief that "education should be free, clear, and within reach," VasmuStudy acts as a digital study companion for self-learners. Whether a student is studying in Patna, Delhi, or a remote village in Bihar, the platform aims to provide the patient, clear breakdown a great teacher would give in a classroom—ensuring no student is left behind because of geography or income.
